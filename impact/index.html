Why a water company cares

Most of the water in these objects was used in factories: making paper and card, glass, plastics, metals and electronics. Much of it was used once and discharged.

That is the water Danu Water works on. When industrial sites can clean and reuse their own wastewater, the hidden water in everyday things goes down.
