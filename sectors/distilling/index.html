Sectors / Distilling

Water and vinasse in distilling

For every litre of rum, a distillery produces many litres of vinasse, known as dunder in the Caribbean. It is strong, salty and hard to treat, and many distilleries are on islands where fresh water is precious.

Where Danu Water fits

These are jobs, not a fixed line-up

The applications illustrate the jobs that are often needed in a distillery. In practice, we design around each site's own combination of problems. Where and how much water we take, and where we return it, is up to you. Often a single unit can do several jobs, or a pair are designed to work together.
