Distilling

Process and cooling water reuse

Less pressure on scarce local water.

The problem

Island and water-stressed distilleries depend on limited supplies for fermentation and cooling.

Benefits

  • Lower freshwater use
  • Less exposure to supply restrictions
  • Reduced discharge

Application

Treats cooling and process water on site so it can be used again.

Where it sits in the distillery

Shown as a standalone job for clarity. Where and how much water is taken and returned is designed around each site, and one unit may do several jobs. Powered by Danu Water's adaptive electromembrane separation.
