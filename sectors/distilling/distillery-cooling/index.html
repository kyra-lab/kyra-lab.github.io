Distilling

Cooling blowdown recovery

Send blowdown back to the tower.

The problem

Cooling towers lose water through evaporation and blowdown, and the blowdown carries the salts that would otherwise cause scale.

Benefits

  • Less cooling tower make-up water
  • Less discharge
  • Less scale in the cooling circuit

Application

Treats blowdown from the cooling tower and returns the water to it.

Where it sits in the distillery

Shown as a standalone job for clarity. Where and how much water is taken and returned is designed around each site, and one unit may do several jobs. Powered by Danu Water's adaptive electromembrane separation.
