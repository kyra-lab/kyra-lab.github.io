Tanneries

Desalting tannery effluent

Take the salt out of the hardest stream.

The problem

Soaking salted hides releases large amounts of chloride, which conventional biological treatment does not remove. High-salinity discharge is increasingly restricted.

Benefits

  • Meets tightening limits on chloride and total dissolved solids
  • Water recovered for soaking and washing
  • A concentrated salt stream rather than a large salty discharge

Application

Treats effluent after conventional treatment, removing salts so water can return to the beamhouse.

Where it sits in the tannery

Shown as a standalone job for clarity. Where and how much water is taken and returned is designed around each site, and one unit may do several jobs. Powered by Danu Water's adaptive electromembrane separation.
