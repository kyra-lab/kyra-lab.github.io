Sectors / Tanneries

Salt and water management for tanneries

Hides arrive preserved in salt, and much of it ends up in tannery effluent. Combined with the chemicals used in tanning, it makes tannery wastewater some of the hardest industrial water to treat or reuse.

Where Danu Water fits

These are jobs, not a fixed line-up

The applications illustrate the jobs that are often needed in a tannery. In practice, we design around each site's own combination of problems. Where and how much water we take, and where we return it, is up to you. Often a single unit can do several jobs, or a pair are designed to work together.

  • Application

    Desalting tannery effluent

    Removes dissolved salts from treated tannery effluent, so the water can be reused and discharge limits met.
