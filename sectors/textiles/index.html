Sectors / Textiles manufacturing

Water reuse for textile dyeing and finishing

Dyeing and finishing use large volumes of water and large doses of salt. Reactive dyeing in particular adds sodium chloride or sulphate by the tonne, and that salt ends up in the effluent. Danu Water separates the salt from the water so both can be used again.

Where Danu Water fits

These are jobs, not a fixed line-up

The applications illustrate the jobs that are often needed in a dyehouse. In practice, we design around each site's own combination of problems. Where and how much water we take, and where we return it, is up to you. Often a single unit can do several jobs, or a pair are designed to work together.
