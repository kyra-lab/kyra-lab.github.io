Sectors / Textiles manufacturing

Reuse 95%+ of your dyehouse water

Dyeing and finishing use large volumes of water and large doses of salt, and the load changes with every recipe. Danu Water turns spent dyebath and rinse water into clean process water, treated to whatever conductivity your process needs, leaving under 5% as a small concentrated stream.

  • 95%+of water returned clean and ready to reuse
  • <5%left over as a small, concentrated stream
  • ZLDless fresh water in and less discharge out, supporting your target

Where Danu Water fits

These are jobs, not a fixed line-up
