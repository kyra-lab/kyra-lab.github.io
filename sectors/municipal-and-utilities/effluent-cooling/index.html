Municipal & utilities

Municipal effluent to cooling water

Keep drinking water for drinking.

The problem

Every cubic metre of drinking water used for cooling is unavailable for homes and new development, while treated effluent, a steady local supply, is usually discharged. Salts and hardness are what stop it being used for cooling.

Benefits

  • Frees potable supply for homes and growth
  • More cycles of concentration and less blowdown
  • By-products such as lime sludge can go to local farms as agricultural lime

Application

Part of a treatment train that takes treated municipal effluent and produces cooling water make-up, with Danu Water removing the salts that limit reuse.

Where it sits in the network

Shown as a standalone job for clarity. Where and how much water is taken and returned is designed around each site, and one unit may do several jobs. Powered by Danu Water's adaptive electromembrane separation.
