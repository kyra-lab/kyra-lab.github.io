Mining & mine water

Mine water for reuse

From perpetual treatment cost to water resource.

The problem

Large volumes of mine water are pumped and treated, at closed sites in perpetuity, and then discharged with nothing recovered. Some waters are chloride-dominated, others sulphate-dominated from pyrite oxidation, so one fixed design does not fit all.

Benefits

  • Turns a perpetual treatment cost into a water resource
  • Tolerates variability between and within sites
  • Copes with solids and scaling minerals that foul reverse osmosis

Application

Sits after conventional dosing and settlement, removing the dissolved salts that stop mine water being reused. Best suited to moderate salinities, particularly sulphate-dominated and mixed waters.

Where it sits in the site

Shown as a standalone job for clarity. Where and how much water is taken and returned is designed around each site, and one unit may do several jobs. Powered by Danu Water's adaptive electromembrane separation.
