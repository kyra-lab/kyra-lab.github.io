Mining & mine water

Designed in for new projects

Within permit from the first day.

The problem

New processing routes, such as acid roasting for lithium, generate concentrated salt side streams by design. Retrofitting treatment later adds cost, risk and permitting delay.

Benefits

  • Never a discharge or contamination risk
  • Feasibility evidence for the permit application
  • Recovered secondary materials that circular economy rules reward

Application

Part of the project's water design from the outset, treating process liquors and recovering salts such as sodium sulphate.

Where it sits in the site

Shown as a standalone job for clarity. Where and how much water is taken and returned is designed around each site, and one unit may do several jobs. Powered by Danu Water's adaptive electromembrane separation.
