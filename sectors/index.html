Sectors

One adaptive platform, applied wherever salts, hardness or metals stop water being reused. Choose your sector to see what we can do and where it fits in your process.

Choose your sector

  • Paper & pulp

    Hard intake water, salts building up as loops close, and metals carried in with recycled fibre.

  • Textiles manufacturing

    Reuse 95%+ of dyehouse water, with salt always removed to your target and colour too where you need it.

  • Tanneries

    Very salty effluent from hide curing and processing, and chromium to keep out of the environment.

  • Laundrettes & laundries

    A complete on-site system that cleans wash water for reuse, so less goes to sewer at trade-effluent rates.

  • Steel

    Cut scale and corrosion across the whole works, raise cycles of concentration, and recover metals from finishing lines.

  • Mining & mine water

    Treat mine water for reuse, polish discharge to meet permits, design water in from day one, and keep metals in leach circuits.

  • Distilling

    Close the water loop in rum distillation: reuse water, potassium fertiliser and more biogas from vinasse and cooling blowdown.

  • Municipal & utilities

    Turn treated municipal effluent into cooling water, and help data centres use harder local water instead of drinking water.

  • Hard water & groundwater

    Soften hard water without salt or sludge, and make well, aquifer and brackish water usable.

  • Desalination

    Push past reverse osmosis' ceiling: more water from every plant and far less brine, without extra heat or chemicals.
