Sectors / Laundrettes & laundries

Water reuse for laundrettes and laundries

Laundries use a lot of water, much of it heated, and most is used once. Reusing rinse and wash water saves both the water and the energy that went into warming it.

Where Danu Water fits

These are jobs, not a fixed line-up

The applications illustrate the jobs that are often needed in a laundry. In practice, we design around each site's own combination of problems. Where and how much water we take, and where we return it, is up to you. Often a single unit can do several jobs, or a pair are designed to work together.
