Sectors / Desalination

Desalination

Brine reduction and extra output

More water, less brine.

The problem

Brine discharge is coming under tighter rules, and reverse osmosis cannot push recovery much further on its own because of scaling and pressure limits.

Benefits

  • Higher overall plant recovery
  • Smaller brine volumes to discharge or manage
  • A concentrated brine that is easier to valorise, for example in construction materials

Application

Treats the RO concentrate, returning recovered water and producing a much smaller, more concentrated brine.

Where it sits in the plant

Shown as a standalone job for clarity. Where and how much water is taken and returned is designed around each site, and one unit may do several jobs. Powered by Danu Water's adaptive electromembrane separation.
