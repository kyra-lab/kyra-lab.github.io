Steel

Recovery from pickling and galvanising

The most concentrated streams on site, put to use.

The problem

Pickling and galvanising produce the most concentrated salt streams in a steelworks, carrying iron, zinc, chloride and ammonium that are costly to treat and valuable to recover.

Benefits

  • Recovered metals and salts instead of waste
  • Less load on wastewater treatment
  • Rinse water returned to the line

Application

Treats rinse and wash water from pickling and galvanising lines to recover salts and metals. Fit is assessed for each stream.

Where it sits in the steelworks

Shown as a standalone job for clarity. Where and how much water is taken and returned is designed around each site, and one unit may do several jobs. Powered by Danu Water's adaptive electromembrane separation.
