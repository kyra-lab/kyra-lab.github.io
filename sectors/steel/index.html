Sectors / Steel

Scale control and recovery across the steelworks

An integrated steelworks runs many different water loops, from intake and gas scrubbers to casting, rolling and finishing. Lowering conductivity at intake, and again at key loops, cuts scale and corrosion throughout the plant. That means fewer forced outages, longer equipment life and less fresh water.

Where Danu Water fits

These are jobs, not a fixed line-up

The applications illustrate the jobs that are often needed in a steelworks. In practice, we design around each site's own combination of problems. Where and how much water we take, and where we return it, is up to you. Often a single unit can do several jobs, or a pair are designed to work together.
