Clear family · Add-on

TraceClear

Extra-low conductivity, where it counts.

The problem

Some uses, including certain boilers and high-purity applications, need water with far lower conductivity than softening alone delivers.

Benefits

  • Extra-low conductivity for the uses that need it
  • Treats only the share of water that needs it
  • Builds on MineralClear, so the two work as a pair

Application

An added step after MineralClear for cases that need extra-low conductivity, including some boiler applications.

Where it sits in the mill

Shown as a standalone job for clarity. Where and how much water is taken and returned is designed around each site, and one unit may do several jobs. Powered by Danu Water's adaptive electromembrane separation.
