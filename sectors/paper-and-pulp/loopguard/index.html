LoopGuard

Close the loop without the build-up.

The problem

As mills reuse more water, salts build up in their process water. The result is scaling, corrosion and process chemistry that stops performing.

Benefits

  • Lower fresh water intake and effluent volume
  • Less scaling and corrosion, so fewer descaling shutdowns
  • More stable wet-end chemistry and lower additive use
  • Deeper loop closure than volume-based treatment allows

Application

Takes treated effluent from the WWTP, removes the salts that limit reuse, and returns the water to the mill. Adaptive electromembrane separation adjusts in real time as effluent quality shifts.

Where it sits in the mill

Shown as a standalone job for clarity. Where and how much water is taken and returned is designed around each site, and one unit may do several jobs. Powered by Danu Water's adaptive electromembrane separation.
