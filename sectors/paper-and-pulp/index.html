Sectors / Paper & pulp

One adaptive platform, four points in the mill

Paper mills face three water challenges: hard intake water, salts building up as loops close, and metals carried in with recycled fibre. Danu Water's adaptive electromembrane separation tackles each where it arises, adjusting in real time as water quality shifts.

Where each product sits

These are jobs, not a fixed line-up

The products illustrate the jobs that are often needed in a mill. In practice, we design around each site's own combination of problems. Where and how much water we take, and where we return it, is up to you. Often a single unit can do several jobs, or a pair are designed to work together.

  • Guard · Internal loop

    LoopGuard

    Recycles WWTP effluent back to the paper machine, without the salts, keeping chemicals effective and avoiding scaling.

    View listing

  • Guard · Internal loop

    BrightGuard

    Removes the metals behind corrosion, deposits and chemistry problems.

    View listing

  • Clear · Intake water

    MineralClear

    Chemical-free softening for hard intake water, giving your process the best quality starting point.

    View listing

  • Clear · Add-on

    TraceClear

    For cases requiring extra-low conductivity, including some boiler applications, this added step comes after MineralClear.
