Industrial water, back in use

Danu Water removes the salts, hardness and metals that stop water being reused. Our adaptive electromembrane separation shifts in real time to handle the variability of industrial water, from a laundrette to a mine site.

Find your sector

Each sector has its own water problems. Choose yours to see what Danu Water can do and where it fits in your process.

  • Paper & pulp

    Hard intake water, salts building up as loops close, and metals carried in with recycled fibre.

  • Textiles manufacturing

    Salt-heavy dyebaths and rinse water that could be reused, if the salts and colour came out.

  • Tanneries

    Very salty effluent from hide curing and processing, and chromium to keep out of the environment.

  • Laundrettes & laundries

    Wash water that could be cleaned and reused on site, cutting water and energy bills.

  • Steel

    Cooling and process water reuse, and recovering valuable ions from steelworks wastewater.

  • Mining & mine water

    Treat mine water for reuse instead of discharge, and manage salt in bioleaching circuits while keeping the metals.

  • Distilling

    Get more from vinasse (dunder): water you can reuse and a potassium-rich concentrate, especially where water is scarce.

  • Municipal & utilities

    Turn treated municipal effluent into cooling water, so industry and data centres stop drawing on drinking water.

  • Hard water & groundwater

    Soften hard water without salt or sludge, and make well, aquifer and brackish water usable.

  • Desalination

    More water from existing plants, and less brine, as discharge rules tighten.

Why it is different

Industrial water is never the same two days running. Most treatment is designed for a fixed condition and struggles when the water changes. Our system measures what is coming in and adjusts how it runs, continuously.

That makes reuse dependable enough to build a process around, and affordable enough that there is no green premium to argue about.

How adaptive electromembrane separation works

  • 95%+ water recoveryfrom industrial wastewater streams
  • 70–80% less energythan zero liquid discharge technologies
  • Continuous and onsitea plug-and-play system, with no tankering or offsite treatment
  • Salts as a resourcea small concentrate stream that can be recovered, not a large dilute waste
