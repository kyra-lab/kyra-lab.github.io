Industrial water, back in use

Danu Water removes the salts, hardness and metals that stop water being reused. Our adaptive electromembrane separation shifts in real time to handle the variability of industrial water, from a laundrette to a mine site.

See it working

Copper sulphate colours the water blue. As our system runs, the salt moves from one stream to the other: the left side gets bluer as it concentrates, and the right side clears until virtually no salt remains.

How it works

Why it is different

Industrial water is never the same two days running. Most treatment is designed for a fixed condition and struggles when the water changes. Our system measures what is coming in and adjusts how it runs, continuously.

We deliver complete systems, not just membrane stacks: designed around your site and your water, with any pre-treatment it needs, and controls that keep it on target without an operator chasing it.

That makes reuse dependable enough to build a process around, and affordable enough that there is no green premium to argue about.

How adaptive electromembrane separation works

  • 95%+ water recoveryfrom industrial wastewater, tuned to each site
  • Low-energy operationno heat and no high-pressure pumping
  • No direct CO₂ emissionsthe system runs on electricity alone
  • Brine reductiona concentrate typically under 10% of the inlet, so disposal energy and costs are dramatically reduced
