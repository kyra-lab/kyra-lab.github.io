Pre-seed round backed by SFC Capital

March 2026

Danu Water closed its pre-seed round in March 2026, backed by SFC Capital.

The round funded the design and build of our industrial pilot units and our first industrial pilot programme. We plan to raise our next round on the back of these industrial demonstrations, to deliver our first commercial orders.
