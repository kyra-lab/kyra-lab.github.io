Startup Battlefield 200 at TechCrunch Disrupt

13–15 October 2026

Danu Water was one of the companies selected for TechCrunch Disrupt's Startup Battlefield 200 in San Francisco.

At booth C2 we put everyday objects on the table, a reusable coffee cup, a power bank and a set of conference giveaways, and asked visitors to guess how much water it took to make each one. Most people guessed far too low.

The answers, and the reasoning behind them, are now on our Understand your impact pages. They are a small way into a big point: water is built into almost everything industry makes, and most of it can be reused.
